Producers & Researchers

How to take part, and at which stage

This program is structured to draw on specialized U.S. capability rather than to hold every function internally. Enquiries are welcome from commercial poultry producers, feed manufacturers and nutritionists, analytical laboratories, university research units and contract research organizations, ingredient suppliers, and regulatory and quality professionals.

Please read first

Participation discussed at this stage concerns research and evaluation activity. It is not the supply of a product for disease-prevention use, and no product is available for purchase. See Regulatory Posture.

What participation looks like, by stage

If you areRelevant stageWhat participation involves
An ingredient supplier Now Quotation and specification discussion for standardized lablab, green tea catechin, garlic, and ginger material; certificates of analysis; marker-compound assay capability; lot consistency and supply continuity.
An analytical laboratory Now Marker-compound method development and validation, bioactive retention assays, stability studies under defined temperature and humidity conditions, mixing-uniformity testing.
A feed manufacturer or mill Now and Year 2–3 Processing-condition consultation, pilot batch production, carrier and premix compatibility, pelleting and conditioning parameters, batch-release process design.
A poultry nutritionist Now and Year 2 Review of candidate inclusion ranges against complete-feed formulation constraints, palatability and intake considerations, ration-integration practicality.
A university research unit or CRO Year 2–3 Protocol development and conduct of safety, tolerance, palatability, and immune- and antioxidant-response evaluation in conventional poultry facilities; statistical design and analysis.
A biocontainment facility Year 3 Any disease-relevant evaluation involving live highly pathogenic avian influenza virus, conducted exclusively at the required biosafety level under approved institutional protocols and applicable USDA authorization.
A regulatory or quality professional Year 1–3 Ingredient-status review, FDA Center for Veterinary Medicine pathway assessment, AAFCO and state feed-control analysis, claim-set definition, labeling review, quality-system design.
A commercial poultry producer Year 4, after preceding validation On-farm practical evaluation of feed delivery, palatability, production parameters, record-keeping, and operational practicality. This follows controlled evaluation; it does not replace it.
An Extension program Any stage Technical review, protocol guidance, laboratory capability, and producer connection.

How enquiries are handled

  • Every enquiry receives a written response identifying the current development stage.
  • Enquiries are recorded in a program log noting date, organization, category, and disposition. This log is part of the program's development record.
  • Technical discussions that require disclosure of formulation specifics proceed under a mutual confidentiality agreement.
  • No participant is asked to endorse, and no participant will be represented as endorsing, product performance that has not been established.
