How we build

Five gated years, from formulation to scale.

We do not skip steps. Formulation stability comes before biological evaluation, and evaluation comes before any claim we would put in front of a customer. The sequence is on the progress page.

Year 1 · In progress

Product definition

Supplier qualification, analytical methods, prototypes, and a stability program.

Year 2

Optimization

Inclusion rates, palatability and tolerance, and repeatable pilot batches.

Year 3

Validation

Controlled evaluation in qualified facilities, then a lawful claim set.

Year 4

Readiness

Producer evaluation, contract manufacturing, and batch-release controls.

Year 5

Scale

Recurring manufacture and distribution through existing feed channels.
