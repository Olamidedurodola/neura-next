Company

A U.S. product-development company with two programs

NeuraNext Solutions LLC is an Arizona limited liability company formed in October 2024. It is the vehicle through which Oluwatunde A. Aderounmu directs product-development work in the United States. The company maintains two programs. They are not the same project, they do not share a regulatory pathway, and they are not interchangeable.

Active product-development program

Feed program

A phytogenic poultry-feed additive in development, intended for study of host-level nutritional support relevant to avian influenza exposure, delivered through existing commercial feed systems. This is the program with a public development record, regulatory posture, and participation channel.

Early-stage research line

Neurotechnology

An early-stage research line concerning spinal-cord injury as a scientific question. It is not a marketed or cleared medical device, and no treatment or clinical-performance claim is made.
